，我要賜給他權柄……來統治萬國，用鐵杖治理它們，像擊碎陶器一樣粉碎它們。我也要把晨星賜給得勝的人。（啟示錄2章26～28節） 主耶穌給推雅推喇教會的信息，直接道出了教會在世俗壓力下的核心挑戰：當面對假先知耶洗碧般的誘惑與社會經濟的排擠，信徒是否仍能堅守對基督的忠貞？在那帝國權力至上的時代，耶穌應許遵行祂命令到最後的人，要賜給他們統治萬國的權柄。這權柄不是源於地上的武力爭奪，而是像耶穌從天父領受一樣，是屬天的、公義的、有最終審判效力的。這權柄如同「鐵杖」，能擊碎敵對上帝的列國，像粉碎陶器一樣。對當時身處社會底層、可能受盡凌辱的推雅推喇信徒而言，這不僅是伸冤的安慰，更是一份主客易位的榮耀應許。 在今日到處充斥戰亂、侵略與地緣政治混亂的世界中，這份應許顯得格外有力量。當我們看見無辜的人民在強權侵略下呻吟、家園被戰火夷為平地，公義似乎在飛彈的硝煙中沉默時，心中難免湧起混亂與無力感。然而，啟示錄的這段經文正挑戰我們，世俗的權力並非最終的存在。當前的侵略者雖然猖獗，但歷史並非終止於此。因為有一位掌權者將要歸來，以絕對的公義重整乾坤。這份應許使我們不被眼前的挫敗吞噬，因為地上的帝國就算再強大，在主基督的鐵杖下也不過是易碎的瓦器。 信仰的省思邀請我們在黑暗中定睛於晨星，這晨星總是在黎明前最深沉的黑暗中出現，預告新一天的開始。主耶穌應許把明亮的晨星──就是祂自己──賜給得勝者，象徵信徒與主最親密的聯合。這提醒我們，面對職場的不義、社會的冷漠和國際的強權欺壓，我們得勝的關鍵不在於效法世界的手段，而在於遵守主的命令到底。這在黑暗的年代看來並不容易，但晨星將帶來盼望。 近年來，世界各地的戰爭常在毫無預警下發生，與此同時，台灣則不斷受對岸有形無形的威脅恐嚇。在全球動盪的年代，主耶穌所應許這份超越今生的盼望，是我們堅守信仰的基礎。教會的信仰團體在地上的影響力或許有限，但我們仍要向這世代見證基督是我們的盼望，使我們能預嘗永恆國度的權柄。無論你此刻正為何事感到心力交瘁，請緊緊抓住基督的應許。黑夜雖然深沉，但聖經真理告訴我們，晨星已經發光。願我們都能在忍耐中，與基督一同站在得勝的一方，直到公義的黎明徹底到來的那一日。

迦勒問他說，你要甚麼。他說，求你賜福給我，你既將我安置在南地，求你也給我水泉。他父親就把上泉下泉賜給他。」（書十五18~19）
押撒的地是「南地」──一塊在烈日之下非常炎熱的地。他父親給了他水泉，從此山上有不竭的泉水流下來使全地涼爽、肥沃了。 我們所在環境，無論如何炎熱、荒蕪、艱難、痛苦、寂寞、平庸，我們總能找到喜樂和祝福的。因為我們的父親已把活水的泉源賜給我們了。 大衛在洗格拉的灰燼中，雖然他妻子兒女都被擄去，眾人說要用石頭打死他，「大衛卻倚靠耶和華」（撒上卅6）。 哈巴谷雖然看見無花果樹不發旺、田地不出糧食….他仍能唱說：「然而我要因耶和華歡欣，因救我的上帝喜樂。」（哈三18） 以賽亞在西拿基立上來攻擊猶大的時候，雖然似乎都搖動到海心了，他仍能用信心唱說：「有一道河。這河的分汊，使上帝的城歡喜….上帝在其中。城必不動搖。」（詩四十六4~5） 許多殉道者在火燄中仍有喜樂；許多信徒在患難中仍有平安；這是為甚麼緣故呢？乃是因為他們能和大衛一同唱說：「我的泉源都在祢裡面。」（詩八十七7） 啊！我們的父親所賜給我們的水泉多麼寶貴！──信宣（A. B. Simpson） 奉獻 Offering

And Caleb said unto her, What wouldest thou? Who answered, give me a blessing; for thou hast given me a south land; give me also springs of water. And he gave her the upper springs, and the nether springs (Joshua 15:18, 19)
There are both upper and nether springs. They are springs, not stagnant pools. There are joys and blessings that flow from above through the hottest summer and the most desert land of sorrow and trial. The lands of Achsah were "south lands," lying under a burning sun and often parched with burning heat. But from the hills came the unfailing springs, that cooled, refreshed and fertilized all the land. There are springs that flow in the low places of life, in the hard places, in the desert places, in the lone places, in the common places, and no matter what may be our situation, we can always find these upper springs. Abraham found them amid the hills of Canaan. Moses found them among the rocks of Midian. David found them among the ashes of Ziklag when his property was gone, his family captives and his people talked of stoning him, but "David encouraged himself in the Lord." Habakkuk found them when the fig tree was withered and the fields were brown, but as he drank from them he could sing: "Yet will I rejoice in the Lord and joy in the God of my salvation." Isaiah found them in the awful days of Sennacherib's invasion, when the mountains seemed hurled into the midst of the sea, but faith could sing: "There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God. God is in the midst of her: she shall not be moved." The martyrs found them amid the flames, and reformers amid their foes and conflicts, and we can find them all the year if we have the Comforter in our hearts and have learned to say with David: "All my springs are in thee." How many and how precious these springs, and how much more there is to be possessed of God's own fulness! --A. B. Simpson

主耶穌應許，凡在祂裡面的，必多結果子。接下去祂又說：「離了我，你們就不能做什麼。」 說來慚愧，因為我們遺傳了亞當墮落的天性——在我們肉體裡面，毫無良善可言，更甚著，可說是與上帝為敵。我們是在罪惡的權勢之下，幾乎無法做絲毫討上帝喜悅的事。 我們需要痛切悔改！多少時候，我們沾沾自喜，以為自己還不錯，也能做些善事。想想主耶穌說的話：「離了我，你們什麼都不能做。」是的，我們只有倚靠祂。 我們該如何感謝上帝！因為耶穌願意住在我們心中，我們也可以與祂聯合，耶穌可以時時刻刻與我們同在，在我們心中做工，賜我們力量，這是屬靈生活的祕訣——耶穌要住在我們心中，幫助我們能完成祂要我們做的事。 多麼令人歡欣鼓舞、雀躍萬分的信息！聖經裡對基督徒的要求似乎是高標準，令人高不可攀，但感謝上帝，因著那穌住在我心裡，祂要助我一臂之力。我只要注意一件事，就是單單地仰望祂，倚靠祂，讓祂在我裡面做工。當我想到「離了我，你就不能做什麼。」我就連想到「那常住在主裡面的，必要多結果子。」 祂要親自來管理這事，就是祂要住在我裡面，我也要住在祂裡面。感謝上帝！永生上帝的靈，要親自來成全此事。感謝上帝！因為基督耶穌的生命在我裡面，我也在祂裡面，那些願意謙卑、堅定信靠上帝的人，聖靈每天每時每刻要引領他，與耶穌更加緊密的契合，享受甘甜美好的團契生活。 奉獻 Offering

The Lord Jesus follows up His great promise that those who abide in Him will bear much fruit, with the words, "apart from Me, ye can do nothing." What a cause for humiliation! Because the nature we inherit from Adam is so corrupt that in us—that is, in our flesh—dwelleth no good thing. Nay, more, our flesh is at enmity against God. We are tinder the power of sin to such an extent that we are unable to do anything well-pleasing to God. What a call to repentance! How often we as Christians have thought that we were able to do that which is good. How often we thought we were making ourselves better. Let us remember Christ's words, "apart from Me, ye can do nothing," and hence¬forth rely only upon Him. What cause for thanksgiving! Christ has united us to Himself, and so dwells within us. This, praise God, is the great work of which the Eternal Spirit will make me capable. Thank God for the life of Christ in me. I in Him, He in me, is the work of the Holy Spirit in each soul that humbly and believingly yields itself for such communion with God.

門徒的憂傷，是因著主的死亡，永遠與祂隔絕；但當主從死亡裡復活，並在他們當中顯現時，他們的憂傷就變為喜樂。信徒所有的憂傷，無論是多深沈，表面上 看起來像是苦毒的泉源，都可轉變成為喜樂。 憂傷越多，喜樂也會越多。若你覺得你的十字架最重，上帝要賜給你的喜樂也越大；你所承受的壓力越沈重，後來經歷的快樂會越多；就像鐘擺的擺動，幅度越大，彈回來的力量也越大。葡萄不經過壓榨，就無法成為美酒，橄欖不經過破碎，香味就無法出來。凡事都要經過對比，才能對照出兩者之間的懸殊，就像鑽石放在黑絨布上，才更顯出它的光彩耀眼！ 來，我的心，莫再沮喪氣餒，過一會兒，你就會脫胎換骨，全人煥然一新。耶穌告訴我們，藉著屬天的煉金術，我們的憂傷能轉變成為喜樂，我不懂這變化是如何產生，但我就是相信，並用期待的心，唱出快樂歌聲，低潮已離我遠去，我要加入晝夜讚美耶和華的行列，我更要高唱，上帝的慈悲救拔我脫離痛苦與折磨。 奉獻 Offering

Your sorrow shall be turned into joy (John 16:20)
Their particular sorrow was the death and absence of their LORD, and it was turned into joy when He rose from the dead and showed Himself in their midst. All the sorrows of saints shall be thus transmuted, even the worst of them, which look as if they must forever remain fountains of bitterness. Then the more sorrow, the more joy. If we have loads of sorrow, then the LORD's power will turn them into tons of joy. Then the bitterer the trouble the sweeter the pleasure: the swinging of the pendulum far to the left will cause it to go all the farther to the right. The remembrance of the grief shall heighten the flavor of the delight: we shall set the one in contrast with the other, and the brilliance of the diamond shall be the more clearly seen because of the black foil behind it. Come, my heart, cheer up! In a little while I shall be as glad as I am now gloomy. Jesus tells me that by a heavenly alchemy my sorrow shall be turned into joy. I do not see how it is to be, but I believe it, and I begin to sing by way of anticipation. This depression of spirit is not for long; I shall soon be up among the happy ones who praise the LORD day and night, and there I shall sing of the mercy which delivered me out of great afflictions.

見所羅巴伯手拿線鉈就歡喜。（撒迦利亞書四：10）
這些小東西表示所羅巴伯手中的工作已經開始了。沒有人能忽視小的開始，因為耶和華已經興起一人，他將堅持下去直至基石已備妥，並聽到勝利的歡呼聲。這對在耶穌裡的每一信徒而言是一大安慰。恩典之工在開始時可能很小，但是線鉈就在那可信靠的手中。比所羅門更偉大的建造師負責建造屬天的殿，祂永不失敗，永不灰心，直到最高的殿頂完工為止。倘若線鉈在一般人的手中，那我們會為建築物擔憂。耶和華的意願卻要由耶穌的手來完成，那我們會為建築物擔憂。耶和華的意願卻要由耶穌的手來完成。倘若牆未經適當之計劃和監工，勿忙砌起，可能會偏斜。但是使用線鉈的是蒙揀選的監督。耶穌總是監督著祂屬靈殿宇的建造，因此它能造得既穩固又美好。我們喜歡速成的結果，耶穌卻寧願有仔細的判斷。祂要用線鉈，凡不在線上的必被拆除。這就是許多諂媚之工失敗和許多閃耀見證被推翻的原因。審判主的教會不是我們的責任，因為耶穌有堅定的手，有真實的眼，祂善於使用線鉈。當為審判在祂手中而歡欣。線鉈被積極地使用，就在營造者的手中。這也肯定地指出祂要推進工作直至完成。主耶穌啊，當我們見到祢在進行偉大的工程時，我們是多麼喜樂！ 奉獻 Offering
